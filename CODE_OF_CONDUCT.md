# Code of conduct

Rhizome Runtime is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and
welcoming collaboration space.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people and their time with respect.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not individuals.
- Make room for questions and different levels of experience.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private information and report security concerns privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, doxxing, sexualized
attention, and publishing another person's private information are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the Rhizome-Project organization owners.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest
avoided in the review.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and project-affiliated communication spaces.
